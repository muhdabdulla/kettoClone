Everything you need to know about starting a campaign or donating on Ketto.

Crowdfunding is a way to raise funds for medical treatment, education, or social causes by collecting small contributions from a large number of people online. On Ketto, you can create a campaign in 2 minutes, share it with family and friends, and withdraw the raised money directly to your hospital or bank account.

You can request fund withdrawal at any time during your campaign. For verified medical emergencies, funds are transferred directly to the hospital's billing desk within 24 to 48 hours.
Yes, donations made to registered NGOs and eligible medical campaigns on Ketto qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. You will receive an instant tax receipt upon successful payment.
To verify a medical campaign, you will need: (1) Hospital estimate letter or medical discharge summary, (2) Doctor's diagnosis certificate, and (3) Patient & campaigner government photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport).
